# Photon Cinematic Concept --- Visual Vision

## 1. Core idea

Photon Cinematic Concept is intended to turn the existing Photon sky
into a cinematic space environment.

The goal is not to make Photon look like another shader.

The goal is to preserve Photon's character while introducing an original
cinematic composition built around a Black Hole, Planet, stars, nebula
and gravitational effects.

## 2. Two sky variants

The concept should support two visual directions.

### Dark Space

A space-black environment inspired by the deep-space appearance of the
IterationT reference.

It should not simply be a flat black color.

It should read as deep space, with enough atmospheric/visual information
from stars, nebula and the Black Hole to maintain depth.

### Photon Purple

A version that preserves Photon's characteristic purple End atmosphere.

The same concept elements should still be present:

-   Black Hole;
-   Planet;
-   stars;
-   nebula;
-   gravitational effects.

The purple variant exists so the edit can retain a distinctly
Photon-like presentation.

## 3. Black Hole

The Black Hole is one of the primary visual anchors.

### Brightness target

The supplied IterationT screenshot is the key reference.

The desired effect is a **blindingly bright emission** around the Black
Hole.

Important characteristics:

-   extremely high perceived intensity;
-   strong bloom/halo appearance;
-   bright emission spreading outward;
-   surrounding space visually affected by the intensity;
-   a clear dark center/event-horizon silhouette;
-   a luminous accretion region that remains visually structured.

The desired impression is:

> The Black Hole is producing an overwhelming amount of visible energy.

It should not look like a normal Minecraft light source placed behind an
object.

## 4. Stars

The star field should feel like actual deep space rather than a sparse
decorative sky.

Target characteristics:

-   high density;
-   full-sky coverage;
-   stars visible across the Void-facing region;
-   varied apparent sizes;
-   varied brightness;
-   subtle randomized twinkling;
-   no obvious repeating pattern.

The stars should support the Black Hole rather than compete with its
brightness.

## 5. Gravitational shadow/light interaction

A future stage will introduce a shadow/light interaction between the
Black Hole and the Planet/environment.

The visual concept is a gravitationally stretched or deformed
shadow-like region associated with the Black Hole.

The effect should feel physically motivated rather than like a
conventional Minecraft shadow.

A major technical requirement is temporal stability.

Flickering is unacceptable.

## 6. Nebula

The planned nebula is a volumetric 3D space feature.

It should not look like a flat texture pasted behind the scene.

It should have spatial depth and should integrate across the
composition, including:

-   upper sky;
-   the broader End/space region;
-   the Void-facing area;
-   lower areas where appropriate.

The nebula should contribute to depth, scale and atmosphere.

## 7. Planet

The current Planet is a supporting large-scale composition element.

For the current development stage, it should remain mostly unchanged.

The Planet becomes important when the gravitational shadow/light system
and final integration are developed.

## 8. Integration

The final visual goal is not a collection of effects.

The systems should influence the viewer's perception of one another:

-   Black Hole brightness affects the perceived surrounding space.
-   Stars exist throughout the same space as the Black Hole and nebula.
-   Nebula occupies real visual depth rather than a flat background
    layer.
-   Planet sits coherently inside the same composition.
-   Gravitational effects visually connect the Black Hole and
    Planet/environment.
-   The purple variant preserves Photon identity while using the same
    cinematic systems.

The desired result should feel like a single environment.

## 9. Reference philosophy

IterationT is used as a visual reference, especially for the Black
Hole's intense brightness and cinematic spatial presentation.

The Photon Cinematic Concept should remain its own visual identity.

Reference images answer:

> "What visual quality are we trying to achieve?"

They do not answer:

> "What code should we copy?"
